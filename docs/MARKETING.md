## Character Specifications

### Spokesperson Description
- **Age:** 30-40 years old
- **Appearance:** Professional, approachable, confident
- **Ethnicity:** Brazilian/Latino or adaptable to target market
- **Attire:** Modern business casual - blazer, clean lines
- **Hair:** Professional style, well-groomed
- **Expression:** Warm, intelligent, trustworthy
- **Body Language:** Open, confident gestures, natural movement

### Voice Characteristics (if generating audio separately)
- **Tone:** Professional yet approachable
- **Pace:** Measured, clear, confident
- **Accent:** Neutral English or light Brazilian accent
- **Energy:** Engaging but not overly enthusiastic

## Visual Style Guidelines

### Color Palette
- **Primary Background:** #050505 (deep black)
- **Surface:** #080808 to #121212
- **Accent 1:** #00e5c0 (teal)
- **Accent 2:** #00d4ff (cyan)
- **Text:** #ffffff (white)
- **Muted Text:** #b0b0b0

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Inter or similar sans-serif, bold
- **Body:** Inter, clean and readable
- **Code/URL:** JetBrains Mono or similar monospace
- **URL Display:** https://www.lambdaapps.dev/

### Visual Elements to Include
- Lambda Apps Λ logo
- Abstract tech patterns (subtle grid, geometric shapes)
- Cloud platform icons (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure) - subtle
- AI/ML visual metaphors (neural networks, data flow)
- Modern office/workspace aesthetics
